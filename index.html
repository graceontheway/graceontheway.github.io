Hi, I'm Grace. I learn things the hard way so you don't have to.

This rubric came out of my own AI learning journey — specifically from trying to figure out why some prompts work brilliantly and others fall completely flat.

The answer turned out to be five words. Before I type a single prompt now, I ask myself:

Who is this for?
What do I need?
Why does it matter?
Feel — what tone or emotion?
Shape — format, length, constraints

Sounds simple. It is. And it works.

The beautiful thing? I built this rubric by having a conversation. You can build your own too. Your AI operating system is already inside you — you just need the right questions to draw it out.

How to use this rubric
  1. Have a conversation with Claude using the Who · What · Why · Feel · Shape framework
  2. Ask Claude to "debrief me" at the end of your task
  3. Come back here and score yourself honestly across five criteria
  4. Paste Claude's feedback into the notes field below your scores
  5. Save — your history builds over time right here
